Lake Tahoe trip · Winter 2026

Atlanta ⇄ Reno · Dec 20–27

Six round-trip options for Mom & Dad, Sunday to Sunday, with no more than one stop each way.

The short version

Prices are per person, round trip, in economy. Checked bags are not included.

These are round-trip flights from Atlanta (ATL) to Reno (RNO) on Sunday, December 20 out and Sunday, December 27 back.

Southwest Basic is cheaper, but seat selection costs extra. Choice includes seat selection, so you can sit together. On Options 1 and 2, Choice is $676.50 per person and Basic is $576.50 per person. Option 3 is $50 more per person ($726.50 Choice / $626.50 Basic).

Top recommendation: Option 1 — Southwest via Nashville, $676.50 per person on Choice. It is the cheapest way to sit together, Nashville is basically on the way, and it lands in Reno earliest, at 2:30 PM.

The week at the lake

This is a starting plan for Sun Dec 20–Sun Dec 27, 2026, based at a South Lake Tahoe rental (exact lodging is not set yet). Stephen, Holly, Robin, Jackie, Lin, Lindsay, Ani, Ayaan, and Laila are driving up from Alameda, about 3.5–4 hours. Mom & Dad fly into Reno; on the top-pick Southwest flight they land at 2:30 PM on Dec 20, then drive about 1 hour 15 minutes to South Lake over Spooner Summit / US-50 or Kingsbury Grade. Their return leaves Reno at 9:05 AM on Dec 27, so they need a very early start from the rental that morning.

Stephen, Ayaan (12), and Laila (11) ski or snowboard Mon, Tue, Wed, and Sunday morning, and may optionally ski Saturday at Heavenly instead of joining the Truckee outing. Holly, Jackie, Lin, Lindsay, Ani, and Robin are the non-ski group. Everyone has plenty to do, without being tied to one nap schedule, and the plan does not assign who stays with Robin. Lin can take a quieter day when she prefers one.

Robin’s naps are approximate and flexible: currently about 9 AM, midday, and mid-afternoon, and he may be down to two naps by the trip. Early dinner stays around 5 PM. Each day leaves a stretch of 90 minutes or more with nothing required. Thursday the 24th is the easy day. Christmas Day hours are often reduced — confirm before you go, and keep a grocery plan for the rental.

Book the timed things first. Sierra lessons and holiday parking sell out; the Dec 24 cruise needs a confirmed sailing time; Heavenly tickets are cheaper bought ahead. Nothing on this plan is booked yet.

Book ahead

  • Sierra-at-Tahoe Wild Mountain Lessons for Ayaan and Laila on Mon Dec 21 — peak rate $366 per child, includes lesson, lunch, lift ticket, and rentals. Book at least 48 hours ahead. A parent must stay on the mountain. Holiday parking reservation required ($25, or free for 4+ in a car).
  • Stephen’s Sierra lift ticket for Dec 21 — adult peak $191.
  • Heavenly lift tickets (and optional lessons) for Tue Dec 22, Wed Dec 23, optionally Sat Dec 26, and Sun Dec 27. Lessons usually do not include a ticket or rentals. Buy ahead; holiday prices are date-based. Saturday at Heavenly is optional for Stephen, Ayaan, and Laila, as an alternative to the Truckee outing.
  • Spirit of Tahoe Emerald Bay cruise for Thu Dec 24 — $105 per person, about 2 hours, heated indoor seating. Confirm the Christmas Eve departure time on the official cruise page.
  • Adventure Mountain tubing for Wed Dec 23 — check current sessions; holiday weeks can be busy. $20 per vehicle plus $40 adults / $35 kids (44" and under) for a 2-hour session.
  • Early dinner at Riva Grill on Dec 24 (same marina as the cruise; confirm Christmas Eve hours) and at Lake Tahoe Pizza Company on Dec 23, and a grocery run for Christmas Day, when many restaurants close or change hours.
  • Call for a Christmas Eve Episcopal service time (2026 times were not posted). Carry chains, and check road conditions before every drive.

Day by day

Sunday, Dec 20 · Arrival

Map: Reno Airport to South Lake Tahoe

Travel day. Everyone meets back at the house. South Lake Tahoe.

Afternoon · Arrive and settle

The Alameda group drives up (about 3.5–4 hours). Mom & Dad, on the top-pick flight, land in Reno at 2:30 PM and drive about 1 hour 15 minutes via Spooner / US-50 or Kingsbury. Plan on arriving mid- to late afternoon, depending on bags and the road. If they are on a later Reno flight, dinner at the house still works.

About 5:00 PM · Early dinner at the house

Everyone meets back at the house for an early dinner. No restaurant outing tonight. Groceries or something simple you bring is enough after a travel day.

Open block: the evening is free at the house. The daily Heavenly Village tree lighting (5:30 PM through Christmas, with live music listed noon–8 PM) moves to Tuesday evening, when the group is already in the village after the gondola. The 3:00 PM Knight Monsters game is the same afternoon Mom & Dad land, so it is not on the plan.

Monday, Dec 21 · First ski day, Sierra

Map: South Lake to Sierra-at-Tahoe

Ski crew in Twin Bridges / Meyers. Everyone else stays in Heavenly Village.

Ski crew · Stephen, Ayaan, Laila

Wild Mountain Lessons, ages 5–13, 9:00 AM–3:30 PM (check-in 9–10, lunch 12–1). Peak $366 per child, gear and lunch included. Stephen stays on the mountain; his adult peak ticket is $191. Optional breakfast at Getaway Cafe in Meyers, 3140 Hwy 50, 7 AM–2 PM, on the way. Bring the parking reservation.

Everyone else · Holly, Jackie, Lin, Lindsay, Ani, and Robin

Morning is open (90+ minutes). After a late breakfast at Heidi’s, 3485 Lake Tahoe Blvd, 7 AM–2 PM, an easy walk around Heavenly Village is enough for the day. Work around Robin’s naps at the house as needed — currently about 9 AM, midday, and mid-afternoon, or possibly two by the trip — without making the whole group wait. Early dinner around 5 PM.

Backups: stay at the house if the village walk does not appeal. Elevated Wine Experience, 4101 Lake Tahoe Blvd, $4 tastings, noon–10 PM daily, is a low-key stop for Lin only (Holly and Jackie do not drink). Ice skating is in the extras if the kids want a later session after they get back from Sierra.

Tuesday, Dec 22 · Heavenly, and the gondola

Map: Heavenly Gondola

The whole group is based at Heavenly Village / Stateline.

Ski crew · Stephen, Ayaan, Laila

Heavenly Mountain. Optional kids’ group lesson to follow yesterday’s first day; lessons usually do not include a lift ticket or rentals. Buy tickets ahead.

Everyone else · Gondola, then village lights

Morning: scenic Heavenly Gondola from 1001 Heavenly Village Way, a 2.4-mile ride with an observation deck (plan 1–2 hours). Ticket prices are date-based; the official ticket pages were not loading when checked, so confirm on the Heavenly site. Weather and wind can close it. Jackie can ride — it is seated. Afternoon after the gondola is the open block (90+ minutes) at the house or in the village, around naps as needed.

About 5:00 PM · Early dinner, then tree lighting

Early dinner in Heavenly Village, then the free daily tree lighting at 5:30 PM, which runs through Christmas. Live music in the village is listed noon–8 PM. This is the lighting that did not fit arrival day. The ski crew can join after they come down.

Open block: the afternoon after the gondola is free. Backups: if the gondola is closed, a short village stroll is enough, then still do dinner and the tree lighting. If the group is tired, skip the lighting and stay at the house. The Nutcracker at Bally’s is 7:00 PM tonight and tomorrow — late for Robin, so only an optional evening for part of the group.

Wednesday, Dec 23 · Heavenly, and tubing

Map: Adventure Mountain, Echo Summit

Ski crew at Heavenly. Tubing at Echo Summit for whoever wants a more active day; others can stay closer to the house.

Ski crew · Stephen, Ayaan, Laila

Second Heavenly day. Last midweek ski day before the Christmas pause.

Tubing, optional · whoever wants a more active day

Adventure Mountain tubing, 21200 Hwy 50 (Nebelhorn), at Echo Summit, about 10 minutes west of town. $20 per vehicle (parking and snow-play areas included) plus $40 adults / $35 kids 44" and under for a 2-hour session. Typical hours 9 AM–4 PM weekdays, 9 AM–8 PM weekends and holidays, conditions permitting. Sledding is not allowed. This is the main active outing; it is optional if they would rather rest.

Optional instead: cross-country skiing, only if someone wants it (Jackie does not snowshoe or ski XC). Closest official option is Camp Richardson Mountain Sports Center, 1900 Jameson Beach Rd, winter rentals when there is snow, typical hours 9 AM–4 PM. Current 2026/27 rental, trail-pass, and lesson prices were not confirmed on the resort site — check with the resort before going. Hope Valley Outdoors and Kirkwood Nordic are farther alternatives; current prices also need a call.

Everyone else, and early dinner

Stay at the house around naps, take a short village walk, or join the tubing as a spectator. Elevated Wine Experience is a low-key optional stop for Lin. Early dinner around 5 PM — Lake Tahoe Pizza Company is a good large-group option tonight, opening at 4.

Open block: late afternoon at the rental before dinner. Backups: if tubing is closed or the pass is messy, stay in Heavenly Village or at the house. Confirm Camp Richardson XC only if they switch to that plan.

Thursday, Dec 24 · Easy day, cruise, and Christmas Eve

Map: Ski Run Marina to St. John’s, Glenbrook

The easy day. One morning outing at Ski Run Marina, a long afternoon at the rental, then dinner and church. No skiing.

Morning · Spirit of Tahoe Emerald Bay cruise

For whoever wants to go — this is a good one for the whole group, including Jackie (seated, with heated indoor cabins). 900 Ski Run Blvd, about 2 hours, $105 per person on the official page (no separate child rate was listed). Confirm the Christmas Eve departure and book ahead on the official cruise page. If a nap lands across the sailing, some of the group can stay at the house.

Afternoon · Open at the rental

The long 90-minute-plus rest of the week. Presents, naps, a movie, or a short walk. Nothing else is scheduled.

About 5:00 PM · Early dinner

Riva Grill, 900 Ski Run Blvd, at the same marina as the morning cruise. The official site lists lunch and dinner daily, with the dinner menu Sunday–Thursday 5:00–9:30 PM. Family-friendly; the official FAQ notes a children’s menu for kids under 10 at $15. Reservations recommended. Confirm Christmas Eve hours on the official page before you book, then leave time for church. Once service times are confirmed, move dinner earlier or later by a little so it still stays an early meal. Backup: eat at the house if the restaurant is closed or the timing does not work.

Evening · Christmas Eve Episcopal service

There is no St. Alban’s in South Lake Tahoe, and St. Francis of the Mountains at Fallen Leaf Chapel is summer-only (roughly May–October). The closer year-round Episcopal parish is St. John in the Wilderness, 1776 US-50, Glenbrook, NV, about 20–25 minutes from Stateline. Regular Sunday worship is at 10 AM; 2026 Christmas Eve times were not posted — confirm on the church site and ask for the earliest family-friendly service because of Robin.

Farther option: St. Patrick’s Episcopal Church, 341 Village Blvd, Incline Village, NV, about 45–60 minutes. Regular Sunday worship is at 10 AM; 2026 Christmas Eve times were not posted on the church site. A 2025 community listing had a mid-afternoon family Eucharist and a later candlelit service there — confirm this year’s times before planning around them. Prefer the Glenbrook parish if they offer an early service, so the drive stays short after dinner.

Backups: if the cruise is cancelled or the lake is too rough, skip it and keep the easy afternoon. If neither Episcopal parish has a workable early service, stay in and have a quiet evening at the rental, or call Lake Tahoe Community Presbyterian Church (2733 Lake Tahoe Blvd), which held 4:00 and 6:30 PM candlelight services on Christmas Eve 2025, as a local backup only. The Granlibakken torchlight parade is in Tahoe City, not South Lake, and is not on this plan.

Friday, Dec 25 · Christmas Day, optional soak

Map: South Lake to Carson Hot Springs

A quiet Christmas at the rental, with an optional afternoon trip to Carson City. Many places close or change hours — confirm before you go. Donner Memorial is closed today. Plan dinner at the rental.

Morning · Slow Christmas at the rental

Presents, breakfast, and naps. No required outing. This is the second easy stretch of the week.

Afternoon, optional · Carson Hot Springs

Picked over Grover for this plan: open 8 AM–9 PM daily, year-round, on a paved, lower-elevation drive of about 45–60 minutes via US-50, with indoor private pools that suit Jackie and a baby better than a mountain-pass soak. 1500 Old Hot Springs Road, Carson City. Outdoor pools, 2 hours: $18 ages 12–54, $16 seniors 55+ and kids 2–11, $8.50 infants under 2, saunas included. Private indoor pools plus outdoor access, 2 hours: $32 / $30 / $24 / $12. No reservations; first come, first served. Confirm Christmas Day hours on the official site. Whoever wants to go can take a 2-hour window; others can stay at the house around naps.

About 5:00 PM · Cook-and-movie night

Grocery shop on Dec 23 or 24. Ayaan and Laila wanted to cook and watch movies together; Christmas evening at the rental is the natural time. Confirm any restaurant you might fall back on; do not count on regular hours.

Backups: skip the soak and have a full easy day at the rental. Grover Hot Springs in Markleeville is the scenic alternative (see extras), but it is only open Friday–Monday in winter, first come, first served, and the drive is over Luther Pass — a worse Christmas Day bet. Indoor ice arena has been closed on Christmas in recent seasons; do not plan on it today.

Saturday, Dec 26 · Truckee and Donner Memorial, optional ski

Map: South Lake to Donner Memorial to Jax

Non-skiers go to Truckee and Donner Memorial. As an alternative to that outing, Stephen, Ayaan, and Laila can optionally ski at Heavenly, which is the closest mountain. The Truckee plan below stays as written for everyone who goes. About 1 hour 15 minutes or more each way. This is a long winter drive with a baby — some of the group can stay in South Lake if that is easier.

Optional · Stephen, Ayaan, and Laila at Heavenly

This is optional, and an alternative to joining the Truckee / Donner outing — not an addition on top of it. Heavenly is the closest mountain if Stephen, Ayaan, and Laila want another ski day. Buy tickets ahead if they decide to go. Anyone who would rather see Donner Memorial and historic Truckee should join the trip below.

Winter driving

Carry chains. Check Caltrans before you leave. The west-shore route on SR-89 past Emerald Bay often closes in storms; the more reliable winter line is usually US-50 to the east shore (NV-28) and CA-267 into Truckee, or another plowed state highway. I-80, 267, and 50 can all have chain control. Leave extra time, and turn back if the road is bad.

Late morning · Donner Memorial Visitor Center

12593 Donner Pass Road, Truckee. Museum exhibits and gift shop are open daily 10 AM–5 PM in winter, closed only for Thanksgiving, Dec 25, and Jan 1 — so it is open today. Plan about an hour. Exhibits cover the Washoe, the emigrant experience and Donner Party, Chinese railroad workers, and early motoring over Donner Pass. The Pioneer Monument is outside. Parking is $10 per vehicle.

Lunch · Jax at the Tracks

10144 W River Street, in historic downtown Truckee, a short hop from the park. Comfort-food diner (burgers, sandwiches, breakfast all day). The official hours list Saturday as 7 AM–8 PM; the homepage also has looser wording, so confirm if you are arriving late. Then walk Commercial Row / historic downtown.

Old Jail Museum

10142 Jibboom Street, one block off Commercial Row. Open only by appointment in winter — contact the historical society through their website if Lin wants a tour. Do not count on a walk-up visit. The Museum of Truckee History in the depot is listed Friday–Sunday 10 AM–4 PM, depending on docent availability, if you have extra time.

About 3:00 PM · Head back

Leave Truckee in time for a 5 PM dinner in South Lake (allow 1 hour 15 minutes or more). Anyone who stayed in South Lake — including the ski crew if they chose the optional Heavenly day — can have a quiet local day: village Ferris wheel and tea cups are listed Dec 26–31, 11 AM–9 PM, $5; ice-sculpture demo 11 AM–5 PM, free; Mario & Luigi 2–5 PM.

Open block: the drive is the long stretch; keep downtown walking short so you are not rushed. Backups: if roads are poor, cancel Truckee and do Mormon Station in Genoa (museum 11 AM–3 PM, closed Tue–Wed, $1, kids 12 and under free) plus a shorter Carson Valley drive, or stay in Heavenly Village for the holiday rides.

Sunday, Dec 27 · Morning ski, then home

Map: South Lake to Reno Airport

Split departure. Mom & Dad leave very early for Reno. Stephen, Ayaan, and Laila ski Heavenly in the morning, then the Alameda group drives home (about 3.5–4 hours).

About 6:00 AM · Mom & Dad to Reno Airport

Allow about 1 hour 15 minutes for the drive over Spooner / US-50 or Kingsbury, plus parking, bags, and security. Leave earlier if there is snow or chain control. Last half-day on the mountain, then back to the rental, showers, and the drive to Alameda. Do not cut it fine if roads look messy. Woody & Jessie character hours (2–5 PM) and Build-A-Bear (4 PM, $20) are after a realistic departure, so they are not on the plan.

Everyone else

Pack, a short village walk if there is time, and ride home with the Alameda group. No second outing.

Backups: if the 9:05 AM plan is too tight after a storm, review the later returns on the Flights tab before booking. If Heavenly is closed or the group is tired, skip the morning ski and start the drive south earlier.

Unscheduled extras

Worth having in your pocket if a day opens up, or as backups. These were researched but are not on the main plan.

  • Heavenly Village Ice Rink, 1011 Heavenly Village Way — optional for Ayaan, Laila, or anyone who wants a skate, not on the scheduled plan. Sessions are about 2–2.5 hours, 10–12:30, 1–3, 3:30–5:30, and 6–8, weather permitting. $25 ages 12+, $20 ages 4–11, under 4 free, rentals included. A fire pit is there for anyone not skating. Indoor backup: Tahoe Ice Arena, 1176 Rufus Allen Blvd ($20 with rental, $16 own skates, $10 under 10, free under 3).
  • Santa photos at Heavenly Village, 5–9 PM through Dec 23, and optional free Story Time with Santa at Village Toys at noon on Dec 22. Not on the scheduled plan.
  • Grover Hot Springs State Park, 3415 Hot Springs Road, Markleeville — the scenic alpine soak, about 30–40 minutes over Luther Pass when the road is clear. Pool complex open Friday–Monday in winter, tickets first come at the park. Vehicle day use $8 ($7 seniors 62+); pool $10 adults, $5 children. Official page does not list session times. Check the official park page for current hours. Carry chains. Held as the backup to Carson.
  • Mormon Station State Historic Park, 2295 Main Street, Genoa — a good history stop for Lin, with an easy half-mile walk for Jackie. Winter museum 11 AM–3 PM, closed Tue–Wed, $1, kids 12 and under free. Pairs with a Carson Valley day if Truckee is cancelled.
  • Elevated Wine Experience or Minden Mill (Thu–Sun 2–8 PM, 21+, $20 wine tasting) for a quieter Lin afternoon.
  • Magic Fusion at The Loft in Heavenly Village, 75 minutes; Sunday 4:30 PM shows on Dec 20 and 27. Ticket price was not listed on the calendar page. The 4:30 Sunday show is the only one that reasonably fits an early dinner.
  • Tahoe Knight Monsters vs. Wichita Thunder, Sun Dec 20, 3:00 PM at Tahoe Blue Event Center — same afternoon Mom & Dad land in Reno, so it did not fit arrival day.
  • The Nutcracker at Bally’s, Dec 22–23 at 7:00 PM; Borges sleigh rides at Sand Harbor (55 minutes, holiday rate $75 adults / $40 children, plus park entry, a long east-shore drive); David Walley’s Hot Springs in Genoa (year-round; day-use price and non-guest access were not listed on the official site — confirm before going).
  • Heavenly Village Cinemas is permanently closed. If the group wants a theater movie, the nearest regular cinema is in Minden; otherwise use the rental.
